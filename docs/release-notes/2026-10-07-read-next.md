# What to read next

canopy-page 0.27.0 ends a page with what to read after it. A page's `readNext:` names it — a path
written as a link from the page, or a quoted `"[[wikilink]]"`, one or a list — on any page of any
section. A stream post always has the list, filled to three: what it names, then its section's
featured posts, then the posts sharing its rarer tags or linked with it, then the section's newest.
[`strings.readNext` and `strings.related`](../reference/settings.md#strings) title it in your words.

A stream section's [`featured`](../reference/settings.md#sections) posts stand atop the first page
of its list, in the order you give, out of the dated pages after it. And a tag's page is now read a
page at a time like the section's list, past `pageSize` posts on `tags/<slug>/page/2.html` ….

`check` names a `readNext:` that leads nowhere and a `featured` entry that is not a post of its
section. The [showcase blog](../showcase/host-blog/index.md) pins a post and names what to read next.
