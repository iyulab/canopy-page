# Lumenfold Journal

Lumenfold is a made-up note-taking product. This section shows a blog standing inside its site:
the header, the footer and the colors are the host's own; the list below, the covers, the authors,
the dates, the reading time, the contents, the tags and their pages, the post pinned atop the list,
what to read after each post, the links on to the next and previous post, the pages of the list,
search and the theme toggle come from canopy-page. How it is put together:
[Hosting a blog in your own site](../../guide/host-site.md).
